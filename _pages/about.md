I am Ji Hyun Byeon, a Ph.D. candidate in Public Administration and Policy at Rockefeller College, University at Albany (SUNY). I am on the job market! My research interests include: 

* Public management
* Government performance
* Interorganizational relations, and
* Regulatory enforcement.

My dissertation investigates the relationship between administrative capacity and performance within the context of U.S. child labor regulations. Utilizing an original dataset on state-level labor enforcement budgets, my work examines (1) how administrative capacity drives regulatory outcomes, (2) how state-level resources interact with federal enforcement performance, and (3) the extent to which capacity allows states to buffer against external challenges in the regulatory environment.
